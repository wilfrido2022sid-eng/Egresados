## Brand & Style

This design system blends the unapologetic kinetic energy, bold graphic contrasts, and mythic parchment textures of shonen manga narrative art with the rigorous, forward-looking engineering precision of an industrial and alternative energy training institution. The design communicates discipline, unstoppable drive, tactical competence, and ecological technological progress. 

The aesthetic is **Tactile Shonen Neo-Brutalism**: heavy ink contours, dynamic angular micro-cuts evoking kunai geometry and solar panel arrays, technical parchment canvas backdrops, high-octane orange ignition accents, and pure chakra-cyan electric diagnostics. It avoids childish tropes in favor of premium editorial manga craft—disciplined layout structures, precise technical telemetry, and visceral kinetic feedback suitable for showcasing apprentices, technical projects, and renewable energy curricula.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around dynamic tension between high-energy solar heat, surgical electric precision, and tactile organic substrate:

- **Primary (`#FF5E00` / Solar Flare `#FF7A00`)**: The blazing signature tone representing relentless will, kinetic output, and solar energy conversion. Applied to primary calls to action, active navigation indicators, and key metric achievements.
- **Secondary (`#111111` / Rich Ink `#1A1A1A`)**: Heavy manga sumi-e ink. Anchors headlines, heavy structural borders, deep headers, and structural technical framing.
- **Tertiary (`#00B4D8` / Chakra Electric `#00E5FF`)**: The current of clean energy, wind turbines, hydrogen cells, and concentrated chakra manipulation. Utilized for data visualization, technical badges, system status indicators, and energy telemetry.
- **Neutral (`#F7F4EB` / Deep Parchment `#EAE5D9`)**: A grounded, warm scroll tone that softens high-contrast elements, providing an organic tactical field that separates the interface from generic digital whites.
- **Vermilion Seal (`#D62828`)**: Reserved exclusively for authorization stamps, rank certifications, and critical safety alerts.

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive hierarchy between heroic visual impact and industrial field instrumentation:

- **Display & Large Headlines (`Bebas Neue`)**: Tall, compact, and unapologetically bold. Evokes Japanese manga cover lettering and bold volume banners. Set strictly in uppercase with tracking to accentuate monumental power.
- **Subheadings & Body (`Space Grotesk`)**: Combines geometric rationality with humanistic quirks, reflecting modern industrial engineering manuals and technical research.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Badges (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Provides monospace computational rigor for scroll metadata, project serial codes, energy yields (kW/h, solar radiation indices), and vocational rank levels. Always uppercase with generous tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (8 columns on tablet, 4 columns on mobile). 

- **Structural Rhythm**: Content sections utilize dynamic asymmetry. Technical data fields and graduate project grids alternate between full-width horizontal scrolls and rhythmic card modules.
- **Speed-Line Framing**: Hero sections and key callouts feature subtle diagonal crop lines or border offsets (e.g., asymmetric 45-degree corner notches).
- **Responsive Adaptations**: Desktop layouts permit heavy 48px outer canvas margins to evoke physical scroll sheets. Mobile screens collapse spacing to a compact, punchy 20px margin, condensing badge stacks into horizontal swipeable tracks while keeping display titles tightly kerned.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects generic soft, blurry drop shadows in favor of **Graphic Ink Elevation (Hard Edge Offset)** and **Tonal Parchment Stratification**:

1. **Hard Ink Offset**: Interactive components and prominent cards use a 2px to 4px hard black drop-shadow with zero blur (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #111111`). When hovered or active, elements translate along this diagonal axis (`translate(2px, 2px)`), creating an authentic comic panel tactile feedback.
2. **Scroll Overlays & Parchment Depth**: Secondary containers use `#EAE5D9` against `#F7F4EB` with crisp 1.5px solid ink-black borders (`#111111`), echoing rolled architectural blueprints and tactical ninja scrolls.
3. **Chakra Overdrive Elevation**: For renewable energy live statuses and breakthrough innovations, cards use an interior hard-edge cyan edge light (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #00B4D8, 4px 4px 0px #00B4D8`).

## Shapes

The shape system is **Sharp (`0`) with Angular Chamfers**. Curves are eliminated to reflect sliced ninja weaponry, industrial manufacturing tolerances, and crystalline photovoltaic wafer geometries:

- **Chamfered Cuts**: Buttons, badge tags, and active tabs feature 45-degree clipped corners (via CSS `clip-path: polygon(...)`) on top-right and bottom-left diagonal pairs.
- **Scroll Edges**: Modal drawers and technical certificates sport double-line vertical end-caps reminiscent of classical scroll spools paired with industrial DIN-rail clamps.
- **Seal Seals & Medallions**: Rank insignias and energy certifications use sharp octagonal stamps or strict geometric diamond rotations.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button**: Blazing Orange (`#FF5E00`) background, crisp 2px solid `#111111` border, 45-degree chamfered top-right corner. Typography: `Bebas Neue`, uppercase with generous tracking. Interaction features a 3px hard black offset shadow that compresses flush on `:active`.
- **Secondary Chakra Button**: Deep ink `#111111` surface with high-voltage `#00B4D8` text and matching cyan hard offset shadow. Used for diagnostics, solar telemetry downloads, and technical datasheets.
- **Tertiary Tactical Ghost**: Transparent surface with `#111111` border, dynamic arrow bracket prefixes (`>>`), and rapid orange text shift on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Ninja Rank / Vocational Level Tags**: Monospaced tags (`GENIN / TÉCNICO`, `CHŪNIN / TECNÓLOGO`, `JŌNIN / ESPECIALISTA`). Bordered in rich ink with high-contrast color fills (e.g., Solar Orange for active projects, Chakra Cyan for clean hydrogen initiatives, Leaf Green for agro-solar).
- **Geometric Seal Badges**: Hexagonal or octagonal vermilion/ink emblem seals containing SENA renewable energy icons (photovoltaic sun, wind vortex, hydraulic turbine).

### Cards (Apprentice & Innovation Showcase)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` or `#EAE5D9` parchment plates bordered by a 2px `#111111` frame with an asymmetric outer drop (`4px 4px 0px #111111`).
- Includes a dedicated tactical header strip showing apprentice rank, project code, and clean energy discipline in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Image containers feature subtle halftone/manga speed-dot textures or dynamic diagonal split frames.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Crisp parchment or stark white background, enclosed in heavy 1.5px ink borders. Focus states shift the border instantly to `#FF5E00` with an electric cyan corner accent dot. Monospace labels positioned strictly outside or as inset stamped tabs.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Strict square checkboxes with sharp corners. Checked states display an energetic diagonal slash or dynamic geometric cross-knot in vibrant orange.

### Technical Data Gauges & Telemetry
- Industrial solar/wind power trackers formatted as horizontal segmented bar meters (chakra energy gauges) bordered by heavy ink lines, transitioning from deep black through cyan to solar orange.
